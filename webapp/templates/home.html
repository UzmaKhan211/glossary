How this works

  1. Upload the spreadsheet -- the master K-5 glossary feedback sheet. This gives every grade its cleaned word list.
  2. Map units -- for a grade, upload that grade's official per-unit glossary PDFs (e.g. Grade5-Unit7.pdf). Each word gets matched to the unit it belongs to, plus its Spanish translation, read straight from the PDF.
  3. Review -- check the matched table, fix anything that needs it, and save.
  4. Export PDFs -- generate the standalone English and Spanish card decks, ready to print.
