How this works

  1. Upload the spreadsheet -- the master K-5 glossary feedback sheet. This gives every grade its cleaned word list. Only needs doing once.
  2. Upload PDFs -- for a grade, upload that grade's official per-unit glossary PDFs (e.g. Grade5-Unit7.pdf).
  3. Map -- match every word against the uploaded PDFs to find its unit and Spanish translation, read straight from the PDF text.
  4. Review -- check the matched table, fix anything that needs it, and save.
  5. Export -- generate the standalone English and Spanish card decks, ready to print.
