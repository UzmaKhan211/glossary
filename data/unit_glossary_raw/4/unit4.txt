D

decimal a fractional value written using single digits for each place value

decimal point a period placed to the right of the ones place

E

expanded form a representation of a number as an addition expression that shows the value of each digit (example: 482 in expanded form is 400 + 80 + 2)

F

factor pair a set of 2 numbers that arrive at a specific number when multiplied (example: 4 and 6 are a factor pair of 24)

H

hundred thousands the base-ten place value unit composed of 10 ten thousands (one hundred thousand in standard form is 100,000)

M

million the base-ten place value unit composed of 10 hundred thousands (one million in standard form is 1,000,000)

multiple a number you get by multiplying another number (example: some multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, and 16)

R

rounding to estimate the value of a number by determining the nearest multiple of a specific place value

S

standard form a representation of a number using only digits to represent place values

T

ten thousands the base-ten place value unit composed of 10 one thousands (one ten thousand in standard form is 10,000)
