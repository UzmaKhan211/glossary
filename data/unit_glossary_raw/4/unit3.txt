common denominator when 2 or more fractions have the same denominator

composite number a number that has more than 1 factor pair

D

denominator the bottom part of a fraction that tells how many equal parts the whole was partitioned into

E

equivalent fractions 2 fractions that represent the same value

M

mixed number a number expressed as a whole number and a fraction less than 1

N

numerator the top part of a fraction that tells how many of the equal parts are being described

P

prime number a number that has only 1 factor pair
