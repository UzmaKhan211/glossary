F

factor pair a set of 2 numbers that arrive at a specific number when multiplied (example: 4 and 6 are a factor pair of 24)

K

kilometer a unit of length in the metric measurement system (1,000 meters is equal to 1 kilometer)

M

meter a unit of length in the metric measurement system (1 meter is equal to 100 centimeters)

milliliter a unit of liquid volume in the metric measurement system (1,000 milliliters is equal to 1 liter)

O

ounce a unit of weight in the U.S. Customary measurement system (16 ounces is equal to 1 pound)
