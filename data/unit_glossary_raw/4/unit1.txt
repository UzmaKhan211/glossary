C

common multiple a number that is a multiple of 2 or more given numbers

composite number a number that has more than 1 factor pair

M

multiple a number you get by multiplying another number (example: some multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, and 16)

P

prime number a number that has only 1 factor pair
